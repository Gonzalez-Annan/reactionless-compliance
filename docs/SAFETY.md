## Contact model

ISO/TS 15066:2016 Annex A body model (Tables A.2, A.3); ISO 10218-1:2025 excludes space robots, so only the body model is borrowed. Two-body energy model v_max = F / √(μ k).
- **free** contact (the person can recoil): F = 2 × quasi-static limit, μ = reduced mass of the arm and the body region.
- **clamped** contact (pinned against structure, e.g. EVA foot restraint): F = quasi-static limit, μ = arm mass.
- Head and face: ISO allows no transient contact; handled procedurally (H3).
- **Arm mass at impact**: the joints are velocity servos saturating at ±5 Nm, so they give way. Simulated impacts (data/safety/impact.csv) show that across the arm the peak force barely depends on the base mass (20 to 1500 kg), but along the arm the joints have no lever arm and the spacecraft mass comes through. Design mass = limp-joint m_eff + 6 kg, blended toward the rigid-joint m_eff with weight (cos of the angle between push and arm)^2. Validated by re-running every impact at the limiter speed (R2).
- **Limiter**: commanded joint rates are scaled so the predicted hand speed stays ≤ 0.85 × v_max(q) for the worst body region and restraint (src.safety.SpeedGovernor, on in teleop and all scripted runs).
