- 2026-10-14 — 3-D RNS solve kept singularity-robust: the stacked [TW·J*; W] is damped (EPS = 1e-6) without rescaling W, which is ~100× smaller than J*, so the reactionless rows soften where the stack loses rank. - 2026-10-14 — Arm mass at impact calibrated by simulated impacts (`python -m src.safety --impact`, data/safety/impact.csv): plate on an ISO tissue spring backed by the region mass (free) or the world (clamped), 216 impacts = 3 servicers × 0/5 kg payload × 3 body cases × 12 random poses/directions, real servos driving at 0.05 m/s and still pushing. Peak force does not depend on the base mass (28 N at 0.05 m/s for 20, 150 and 1500 kg bases): the ±5 Nm servos give way, so the rigid-joint m_eff (154 kg medium, 1495 kg large along the tool axis — the whole spacecraft) is far too pessimistic, and the limp-joint m_eff (~3 kg) too optimistic (force 35 % higher). Design mass = limp m_eff + 6 kg (SERVO_MASS): conservative for 215/216 impacts, worst miss 15 %; the limiter at 0.85 × v_max covers all 216. A multiplicative factor fitted badly (ratio up to 6.5 when the limp mass is small) — P3 for P4.
